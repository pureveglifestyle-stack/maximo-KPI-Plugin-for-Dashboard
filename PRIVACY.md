# Privacy

* OpsLens collects **no** personal data and sends **nothing** to its authors or to any third party.
* Maximo data is fetched directly from your Maximo server into your browser. It is kept in memory only while the
  dashboard tab is open.
* The extension stores only these items in `chrome.storage.local` on your device: whether you accepted the terms
  (with the version and a timestamp), and your display preferences (theme, period, auto-refresh).
* There is no analytics, tracking, telemetry, advertising or remote code. All scripts ship inside the extension.
* To remove everything, uninstall the extension.
